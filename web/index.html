      Cars: simple shapes in team colours with the driver's three-letter code, no logos or liveries; each team's second car has a white stripe. Feed positions have irregular timestamps (±0.1 s), so they are smoothed over ~2 s windows before the replay. Positions, laps, order, pit stops and flags from OpenF1 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The feed puts all cars on almost the same line (median spread 0.2 m): that line serves as the track centre, and cars side by side are spaced apart only so they don't overlap. Pit lane derived from pit-lane passes. Buildings, trees, water and roads from Overture Maps (© OpenStreetMap, ODbL); elevation from Terrarium (AWS Open Data). Unofficial, non-commercial project.
